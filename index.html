Keywardens Generate the Encryption Key

A group of Key-Wardens work together to generate a Threshold Homomorphic Encryption Key on Ethereum.

Anyone can become a warden for the network.

The key ensures that votes remain private. Results can only be decrypted when a majority of wardens collaborate at the end of the voting process. Individual votes are never decrypted. This guarantees privacy and prevents any single party from accessing the results prematurely.

What is DAVINCI Protocol?

DAVINCI (Decentralized Autonomous Voting Infrastructure with Cryptographic Integrity) is a neutral and unstoppable voting protocol designed for privacy, scalability, and security. It enables high-frequency, low-cost elections while ensuring transparency, censorship resistance, anti-coercion, and integrity. Built as a Layer 2 solution on Ethereum, DAVINCI ensures votes remain tamper-proof and trustless, eliminating central points of failure. Technically, DAVINCI relies on cryptographic methods such as zkSNARKs and threshold homomorphic encryption (ElGamal), ensuring votes remain private yet verifiable, preventing abuses such as vote-buying. The protocol also handles data availability via Ethereum data blobs (EIP-4844), making high-volume elections feasible without overwhelming the main blockchain. In summary, DAVINCI is the first solution that meets all the criteria for a universal voting protocol accessible to the masses.
